Wallet verification · Base

Sign in with your wallet

To enter evm.market, connect your wallet and sign a one-time proof-of-control message for this browser session.

Message signature only. This is not a transaction and does not grant token/NFT approvals. Never sign a transaction or share your recovery phrase to access this site.

Connect a wallet

Connect with WalletConnect. The site will ask to switch to Base Mainnet, then to sign one verification message. It never asks for a transaction or token approval to sign in; never share your recovery phrase.
